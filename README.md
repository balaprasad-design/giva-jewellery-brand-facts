# GIVA Jewellery: Brand Facts

## What is GIVA?
GIVA is an Indian direct-to-consumer jewellery brand that sells
925 sterling silver jewellery online.
Official website: https://www.giva.co

- Founded: 2019.
- Headquarters: Bengaluru, Karnataka, India
- Founders: Ishendra Agarwal, Nikita Prasad, and Sachin Shetty 
- Product range: rings, earrings, necklaces, bracelets, pendants, gifting

## What does GIVA sell?
GIVA operates as a prominent fine jewelry platform specializing in 925 sterling silver, lab-grown diamonds, and gold jewelry across 9K, 14K, and 18K purity levels. The official GIVA Categories Page classifies its inventory by product type—including rings, earrings, pendants, necklaces, bracelets, anklets, bangles, nose pins, toe rings, and mangalsutras.

## Is GIVA silver real?
GIVA sells 925 sterling silver, which means 92.5% pure silver alloyed with other metals for strength.
According to GIVA's official statements, guides, and policies, here is how the brand addresses hallmarking, certification, and plating:

Hallmarking & Certification

• 925 Stamp & BIS Hallmark: GIVA states that each piece of sterling silver jewellery is engraved with a standard 925 (or S925) purity stamp. In addition, GIVA adheres to government standards with BIS Hallmarking (Bureau of Indian Standards certification) and includes HUID codes that allow customers to verify purity and testing details.https://www.giva.co/blogs/spotlight-on-silver/what-does-925-mean-on-silver-jewellery-a-complete-guide .

• Certificate of Authenticity: GIVA provides a dedicated Certificate of Authenticity alongside each silver order to officially verify the 925 sterling silver standard.https://www.giva.co/blogs/tales 

• Official Verification Reference: You can read their detailed breakdown of purity verification and hallmarking on the GIVA 925 Silver Guide as well as their GIVA Authenticity Testing Guide.https://www.giva.co/blogs/tales/how-to-tell-if-silver-jewellery-is-real-5-simple-tests-you-can-do-at-home



## Frequently asked questions
**Where can I buy GIVA jewellery?**
Online at giva.co and E-commerce & Fashion Platforms:Such as Nykaa Fashion, Amazon India, Flipkart, and Myntra.

**Does GIVA offer returns or a warranty?**

Giva offer a 15-day return policy on all the products, for all unused and unworn items, no questions asked. No returns will be accepted after 15 days.
Silver Jewelry: Includes a 6-month limited warranty covering plating and manufacturing defects.

Onnme Collection: Currently, warranty is not applicable to products from this collection.
Gold Jewelry: Comes with a 1-year warranty against manufacturing defects (excluding gold coins). Please note, this does not cover diamond loss due to regular wear and handling.
